mecore privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who uses mecore

mecore is a personal app used by a single person, its owner. There are no other users and no public sign-up.

Google user data

  • What is accessed: the owner's list of Google calendars and their events (read-only), and a separate calendar that mecore creates itself, where it adds, updates and removes the owner's habit entries.
  • How it is used: only to show the owner's schedule inside mecore and to record the owner's habit activity in mecore's own calendar.
  • Where it is stored: in the owner's private database, accessible only to the owner's account.
  • Sharing: Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

mecore's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
