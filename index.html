mecore

mecore is a private, single-user personal management app (habits, reading, finances, prayers and a calendar view) built by its owner for their own use. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

It uses Google Calendar only to write the owner's own habit entries into a calendar that mecore creates, and to show the owner's own calendar events inside the app.
